# Voice Standards & Language Rules

## Canonical Phrases
Luxury Fine Art Portrait Photography, Commissioned Artwork, Commissioned Heirloom Portrait Artwork, Museum-Quality Portrait Artwork, Museum-Quality Artwork, Collectable Paintings, Fine Art Portrait, Classical Composition, Narrative Storytelling, Legacy Preservation, White-Glove Art Experience, Museum-Quality Craftsmanship, Certificate of Authenticity, Provenance, Artist Commission, The Hersberger Method®.

## Voice Profile
Authoritative, Refined, Museum-inspired, Timeless, Precise, Collector-oriented, Legacy-forward, Educational, Craftsmanship-driven, Emotionally sincere without sentimentality.

## Tone Markers
Masterpiece Artwork speaks with the voice of an artist, museum curator, art historian, and master craftsman—not a commercial photography business. Language consistently presents each commission as a collectable work of art, a cultural artifact, and a historical record.

## Language Rules
- **Photography Hierarchy**: Photography is always described as the artistic medium. Artwork is always described as the finished product.
- **Preferred Language**:
  - Instead of "We sell photography." → "We create museum-quality portrait artwork through luxury fine art portrait photography."
  - Instead of "Book a photo session." → "Begin your commissioned portrait experience."
  - Instead of "Receive photographs." → "Collect your commissioned artwork."
- **Avoid**: Positioning photography as the finished product. Reducing commissioned artwork to commodity photography. Emphasizing volume, speed, discounts, or disposable imagery.
